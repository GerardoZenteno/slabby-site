Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Sloper ("Sloper", "we", "us") is a climbing-terminology learning app operated by Gerardo Zenteno Gaeta, an individual developer based in Mexico. This policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, and your choices. By using Sloper, you agree to this policy.

Contact: zega.apps.dev@gmail.com

1. Information We Collect

Account information

  • Email address and password when you register with email. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are never visible to us.
  • If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name (if shared), email address, and a unique account identifier from that provider. With Apple, you may choose to hide your email.

Onboarding information (provided by you)

  • Your climbing level, lead climbing level, years of climbing experience, planned daily time in the app, and your selected goals.
  • We use these answers to calculate a climber profile level, which is shown in your profile.

Learning and usage information

  • Progress through the Route, flashcard results and spaced-repetition data, Gym game results, category progress, streaks, badges, and most-practiced terms.
  • Your language preference.

Subscription information

Subscriptions are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play and managed through RevenueCat. We receive subscription status, product purchased, purchase and renewal dates, and an app user identifier. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

Technical information

  • Firebase Analytics: app interactions (e.g., screens viewed, features used), device type, operating system, app version, language, approximate location derived from IP address, and device or app-instance identifiers (such as the advertising ID where permitted by your device settings).
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports and diagnostics (e.g., device model, OS version, app state at the time of a crash).
  • Push notifications: if you consent, we collect a device push token to send you a weekly reminder to do your daily training. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.
We do not knowingly collect precise location, contacts, photos, or health data.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and secure your account and authenticate you.
  • To determine your climber level and personalize your learning path.
  • To provide the Route, Gym, Board, streaks, and badges, and to sync your progress.
  • To verify and manage your subscription and restore purchases.
  • To send weekly training reminders by push notification, only if you have consented.
  • To understand how the app is used, fix crashes, and improve it (Analytics and Crashlytics).
  • To communicate with you about support requests or important service changes.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

Legal bases (EEA/UK users): performance of our contract with you, our legitimate interests in operating and improving the app, compliance with legal obligations, and consent where required.

3. Advertising and Sale of Data

Sloper contains no third-party advertising and no ad mediation. We do not sell your personal data or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

4. Service Providers

We share data only with providers needed to run the app, under their own privacy terms:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Analytics, Crashlytics, Cloud Messaging) – authentication, data storage, usage analytics, crash reporting, and push notifications.
  • RevenueCat – subscription management and entitlement verification.
  • Apple and Google – sign-in and payment processing.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect rights, safety, or security.

5. International Transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. Data Retention

We keep your account data (email, onboarding answers, and learning progress) for as long as your account is active, as it is needed to provide the service. When you delete your account, we delete this data from our database. Analytics and crash data are retained according to Firebase's standard retention settings and are not linked to your deleted account beyond those periods. Subscription records held by Apple, Google, or RevenueCat are subject to their own retention policies.

7. Your Rights and Choices

  • Delete your account: in the app, go to Profile → Danger Zone → Delete Account.
  • EEA, UK, and similar jurisdictions: you may have the right to access, correct, delete, export, or restrict the use of your data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.
  • California and other US states: you may have the right to know what personal data we collect, to request access, correction, or deletion, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. We do not "sell" or "share" personal data as defined by the CCPA/CPRA.
  • Mexico: you may exercise your ARCO rights (access, rectification, cancellation, and opposition) under applicable Mexican data protection law.
  • Notifications and analytics: you can turn off push notifications in your device settings and reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at zega.apps.dev@gmail.com. We will respond within the time required by applicable law.

Subscriptions: manage or cancel in your Apple ID or Google Play settings. Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures, including encrypted connections and Firebase security rules. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

Sloper is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. In some countries, the minimum age to consent to data processing is higher than 13 (up to 16); where this applies, users below that age may use Sloper only with the consent of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child under the applicable age has provided us with personal data, contact us at zega.apps.dev@gmail.com and we will delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. We will post the new version here with a new date and, for material changes, notify you in the app or by email.

ACCOUNT MANAGEMENT

Delete Your Sloper Account

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Sloper is developed by Gerardo Zenteno Gaeta. You can request deletion of your Sloper account and all associated data at any time, whether or not you currently have the app installed.

Contact: zega.apps.dev@gmail.com

warning Important Notice

Account deletion is permanent, immediate, and cannot be undone. All your progress, climber level, spaced-repetition history, streaks, and badges will be permanently erased.

smartphone Option 1: Delete your account from within the app (recommended)

  1. Open the Sloper app and log in.
  2. Go to Profile → Danger Zone.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the action when prompted.
Immediate Effect: Your account and all associated data are deleted immediately upon confirmation.

mail Option 2: Request deletion by email (if you no longer have the app installed)

Send an email to zega.apps.dev@gmail.com from the email address associated with your Sloper account, with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".

Please include the email address linked to your Sloper account so we can locate and verify your account. We will process your request and delete your account and data within 5 business days, and will confirm by email once completed.

delete_forever What data is deleted

When your account is deleted (via the app or by email request), the following data is permanently removed from our systems:

Data Deleted?
Email address and authentication credentials (Firebase Auth / Google / Apple) Yes, immediately
Account identifiers Yes, immediately
Onboarding information (climbing level, experience, goals) Yes, immediately
Learning progress (Route progress, flashcard spaced-repetition data, streaks, badges) Yes, immediately
Gym game results, most-practiced terms, and user preferences Yes, immediately
Push notification tokens Yes, immediately

shield Data that may be retained

We do not retain personally identifiable data after account deletion. We may retain anonymized, aggregated usage data (which cannot be linked back to you) for analytics purposes, as described in our Privacy Policy. Third-party services we use (such as Google Play Billing or Apple App Store, for purchase records) may retain transaction data separately, in accordance with their own retention policies and applicable law.

Important note about subscriptions: Deleting your Sloper account does not automatically cancel an active paid subscription. Subscriptions must be canceled through your Apple ID or Google Play settings.
